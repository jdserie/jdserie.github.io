Research notes

Scope and access

Fifteen DEATH BATTLE episodes were selected with 30 distinct fighters. Written analysis is based on the YouTube captions, with official franchise links and character leads where available. Full video playback could not be verified in the research environment.

Generation 1 identifies the episode and reads the available transcript. Generation 2 follows the feat, version, equipment, and counter claims. Generation 3 traces those claims toward publisher records, manga chapters, game materials, or the closest source located. Unchecked chapters, paywalled evidence, fan interpretations, and untraced franchise leads remain open.

Episode verdicts are the show's interpretations. The 2.0 rating uses the stated profile and arena rules. Favoring the same fighter is neither a validation requirement nor an independent accuracy test.
